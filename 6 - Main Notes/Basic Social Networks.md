# Basic Social Networks
“A social network is a set of actors that may have relationships with one another. Networks can have few or many actors (nodes), and one or more kinds of relations (edges) between pairs of actors.” Hannemann, 2001


### Brief History
- 17th century: Spinoza developed first model
- 1937: J.L. Moreno introduced sociometry; he also invented the sociogram
- 1948: A. Bavelas founded the group networks laboratory at MIT; he also specified centrality
- 1949: A. Rapaport developed a probability based model of information flow
- 50's and 60's: Distinct research by individual researchers
- 70's: Field of social network analysis emerged.There were new features in graph theory that gave us more general structural models. There also was better computer power allowing analysis of complex relational data sets
Early social web, Web 2.0
- New wave of Internet applications since 2000 that introduces the web as a platform for sharing and collaborating. There were also new application paradigms and technologies: Blogs, Wikis, RSS, Podcasting, AJAX,  tags and folksonomies. The dynamic content controlled by users not webmasters cultivating content
Social networks
- Fuzzy – best characterized by example:
	- Flickr, Google maps, del.icio.us, digg, last.fm, MySpace

### Representation
Since we can model our social networks as nodes and the connections between nodes definitionally, we are able to use a wide variety of graph algorithms to analyze our networks.
