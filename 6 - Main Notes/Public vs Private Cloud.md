# Public vs Private Cloud 

## Public Cloud
This means that all of our resources are hosted on the public cloud and we have no local resources. Typically hardware is shared hardware with other customers. The advantages is that there's no capital expenditure, high availability and agility, pay as you go pricing, no hardware maintenance, no technical skills needed. The disadvantages are security, ownership, microsoft pricing, and potential business requirements such as legality.

## Hybrid Cloud
Combines public and private cloud in order to get the most flexibility possible. Legacy apps can be run in private cloud, utilize existing infrastructure, and meet security requirements by using owned servers. The disadvantages include cost, the complexity to manage, and mandatory IT skills.
## Private Cloud
You host everything in your own data center and it is self serviced. Advantages are that you maintain everything and can support any scenario, total control over security, and can meet any compliance standards. The disadvantages are lack of agility, initial CapEx, and mandatory IT skills.
