The first image I chose was a meeting that took place between New York Financier Jeffrey Epstein and Steve Bannon that was released by the democratic house oversight committee.  There are many concepts at play in this image, including Bourdieu's capital conversion and the idea of structure and agency. Through his initial large economic capital he was able to generate leverage and meet with famous people including Steve Bannon at this meeting. By doing this he was converting his economic capital to political and social capital. Furthermore, both men are exercising agency by willingly attending this meeting but it is shaped by broader structures such as wealth, poltiical influence, and networking among elites. The meeting therefore shows how individuals with resources can use their agency within existing power structures to expand their influence and access to other forms of capital.

The second image I chose was from a No Kings protest in my camera roll. There are many concepts at play here but I chose to focus on the feelings of anomie and class conflict. Anomie, a term popularized by sociologist Émile Durkheim, refers to a state of normlessness in society where shared values and standards break down, leading to instability and disconnection among individuals. If this doesn't describe the current political climate in America then I can't imagine what does. Furthermore these protests exist in the context of a growing anger against the richest men in the world. These men are openly giving bribes to the President, and are clearly receiving favors leading to examples of class conflict such as no kings and the shooting of healthcare CEO Brian Thomas by Luigi Mangione.
