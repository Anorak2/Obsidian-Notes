Pick a random animal, and research at least five interesting facts about this animal. Write a short story centered around one of these interesting facts. 



Meerkat:
- Known for standing upright in order to watch for predators
- live in packs of 3-25 with overlapping homes
- they forage for food, mostly small, but larger food they beat with the claws on their forefeet.
- Meerkats have a social hierarchy, and a number of different calls for communicating to each other for reasons such as warning about predators
- mobs of meerkats will fiercely attack snakes, and they have predators such as eagles, hawks, foxes, and jackals.

	It's a hot day in the Savannah and at this time most Meerkats would be resting to conserve energy, but today Runner's on guard duty. Runner is a little smaller than the average meerkat, and he tends to get picked on as a result. One thing Runner does have is that never once has he lost a race, and he prides himself on it. Running is one of his favorite things and he runs at every chance, but today Runner's on Guard duty. The only thing that helps him pass the time is shifting between the several large rocks most use on watch as fast as he can, only pausing for a moment to scan his surroundings at each. 
	Normally this works pretty well, and it helps the few hours of watch duty go by a lot faster. Today however, is different, as when he's doing his route he sees an unusual shape in the distance. This is pretty strange, but quickly bored he returns to running his route. Yet as he finishes his loop again the shape is closer. This is very unusual, and it stops him. Unsure, and with his vision obscured by the rocky ground, Runner adjusts to stand on his hind legs. As he does so he locks eyes with a furry mass, ears pricked, moving slow yet with tensed legs. Heart beating faster than ever, Runner tries to turn back. His burrow isn't too far, and as the fastest meerkat he knows it shouldn't be an issue beating the jackal. This all makes perfect sense, but as he goes to run his body doesn't move with him. He makes the predator cry, half warning the others and half trying to convince himself to move, yet he can't. His muscles feel ready to explode with adrenaline, but his feet have sank into the stone. The fear materializes in his bones and his body begs to move, but his feet are stuck in the stone. For all of the speed he prides himself on, and his thoughts rushing, he can't help but watch the jackal crest the rocks. The last thing that runs through his mind is that if only he ran more, this whole thing could have been avoided.
